Forty million dollars to give two million people safe water. But when does the water actually arrive?
The promise: one dollar, one year of clean water. Two million people, for decades.
But wells, pipes and permits take time. The first communities are expected online in 2026 and 2027.
Meanwhile, families still need safe water today. Boiling needs fuel. Filters need upkeep.
AquaCore's chlorine dioxide tablets treat twenty-five litres in thirty minutes, with no construction at all.
Infrastructure for tomorrow, safe water for today. It's not one or the other. It's both.
