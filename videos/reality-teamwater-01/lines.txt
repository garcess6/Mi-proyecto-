Adin Ross wants an audit of the forty million dollars raised for Team Water. So we checked the numbers.
You have millions for water? True. Over forty million dollars in one month, through WaterAid.
A water park? Not quite. The goal is two million people with safe water, for decades.
So far? His one-year update says about two hundred and twenty-five thousand people. Around eleven percent.
Built so far: a solar water farm in Tanzania, filters in Brazil, and pipelines in Rwanda, still under construction.
Real money, real projects. But most of the impact is still to come, and a public, independent audit would settle the debate.
