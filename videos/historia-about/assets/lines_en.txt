We're AquaCore. Safe water starts with what you can't see.
Water can look clear, and still make people sick. Millions of families drink from rivers, wells and rain tanks that are never tested or treated.
So we made water treatment simple: chlorine dioxide tablets, and a local network to deliver them.
Fill a twenty-five litre container. Add one tablet. Wait thirty minutes. Ready to drink and cook.
Tested by national health labs in Africa, against E. coli, Salmonella and cholera.
We work with NGOs, governments and investors, worldwide.
