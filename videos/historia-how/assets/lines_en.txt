How does it work? Safe water, in four simple steps.
One: fill a clean, covered, twenty-five litre container, with water from a well, a river or a rain tank.
Two: add one tablet.
Three: wait thirty minutes, and let the tablet work.
Four: your water is ready for drinking and cooking.
One tablet, twenty-five litres. Results depend on the source water and correct use, so always follow the instructions.
