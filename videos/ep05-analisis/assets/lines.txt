You can't see germs. So how do you know if water is safe? You test it.
Step one: take a sample. A clean, sealed bottle, kept cool, and analysed the same day.
In the field, quick kits and test strips check things like chlorine, pH and nitrates, in minutes.
In the lab, samples are grown on plates, and each colony is counted. For safe drinking water, there should be zero E. coli in a hundred millilitres.
The key is before and after: test the raw water, treat it, then test again.
That's how national labs in Cameroon and Niger tested AquaCore tablets: before and after treatment.
