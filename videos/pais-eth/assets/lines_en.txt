Fifty-nine million people in Ethiopia don't have even basic drinking water. That's more than the entire population of Spain.
More than eight in ten Ethiopians don't have safe water at home. In rural areas, over half lack even basic water: the source is more than thirty minutes away, or unprotected.
Unsafe water spreads cholera, typhoid and diarrhoea. In 2024 alone, Ethiopia reported over twenty-seven thousand cholera cases, and two hundred and seventy-one deaths, in an outbreak that has lasted since 2023.
Ethiopia has a national plan to eliminate cholera by 2028, with surveillance, vaccination, and water and hygiene programmes, working with WHO and partners.
What can families do today? Boiling needs fuel. Filters need upkeep. Chlorine struggles in cloudy water.
AquaCore's chlorine dioxide tablets: one tablet treats twenty-five litres in thirty minutes, works in cloudy water, and in tests by national labs in Africa, it eliminated E. coli, Salmonella and cholera bacteria.
