How does a pilot work? Five steps, from a first test to full deployment.
One: we send a hundred tablets to your country.
Two: your local lab tests the water, before and after treatment.
Three: we review the results together.
Four: we size the program. How many people, how many tablets.
Five: we deploy, with local distribution and training, and scale, region by region.
