- **Título del Reel:** What happens when you drink contaminated water? 🦠 | EP. 2

**Texto (caption):**
```
It looks clean. But what happens next? 🦠

If water carries germs (bacteria, viruses or parasites), within hours or days you can get stomach pain, vomiting, diarrhoea and fever. The real danger is dehydration, especially for young children and older people.

Contaminated water can also spread diseases like cholera, typhoid or hepatitis A.

And some contaminants, like arsenic, nitrates or lead, don't make you sick today. They build up silently, over years.

No smell. No colour. No warning. That's why water should be tested and treated before you drink it.

## EP03 · Clear Is Not Safe
- **Vídeo:** `AquaCore_EP03_Clear-Is-Not-Safe_9x16.mp4` (42,8 s)
- **Portada:** `AquaCore_EP03_portada.png`
- **Título del Reel:** Clear ≠ safe: your eyes can't tell you 👀 | EP. 3

**Texto (caption):**
```
Clear ≠ safe. 👀

Germs are tiny: hundreds could line up across a pinhead. Clear water can still carry them, and cloudy water can hide them and make treatment work less well.

Your eyes, nose and taste can't tell you if water is safe. So how do you spot the risk?

⚠️ Look for warning signs: toilets or animals near the source, open tanks, cracked pipes, floods or heavy rain.
🧪 Test it: a lab or a field kit can detect E. coli (a sign of faecal contamination) and chemicals like nitrates or arsenic.
📅 Test again over time: water quality can change with the seasons.

💬 How do YOU treat your water?
